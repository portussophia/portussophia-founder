This page presents a professional portfolio, not the complete PortusSophia research catalogue. Programme-level dependency maps, reading paths, methods, and active research records belong on the [public programme site](https://public.portussophia.com/research).

## Foundational and methodological work

### The PortusSophia Field Guide

A practical orientation to the architecture, boundaries, and working vocabulary of PortusSophia. The Field Guide introduces a way to encounter cross-field work while keeping provenance and dependency visible.

### Contemplation

An inquiry into attention, reflection, and the conditions under which thought can remain answerable to both experience and method.

### Philosophy of Science companion inquiries

A connected series examining translation, representation, models, evidence, and explanation across fields. These works are intended to be read as companion inquiries rather than claims of a completed universal system.
