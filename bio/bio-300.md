James Roy Dennis is an independent researcher, independent scholar, author, and the Founder of PortusSophia, LLC. He develops provenance-centered forms of inquiry for questions that exceed the explanatory reach of a single discipline, profession, or institutional frame.

His work is concerned with how claims, methods, categories, and evidence move between fields. Cross-field work can produce insight, but it can also conceal dependencies, flatten differences, and grant conclusions more authority than their sources permit. James’s research asks how those encounters can remain useful while preserving origin, transformation, limits, and accountability.

That concern has an applied lineage. Experience with systems, terminology, documentation, healthcare interoperability, engineering, operations, and high-consequence environments made boundary failures tangible. Information may be technically transmitted while meaning is degraded; a visible failure may be separated from its originating condition; a procedure may be followed while its governing purpose disappears. These continuities inform his emphasis on traceable reasoning, bounded authority, and accountable human judgment.

James treats AI and related computational tools as instruments within inquiry. They can assist discovery, comparison, mapping, and drafting, but they do not displace authorship, source evaluation, or responsibility for judgment.

His books, papers, companion inquiries, and public work form the human provenance of PortusSophia™. PortusSophia is a field architecture that makes cross-field encounter governable. Its declared interface, distributed research, methods, reading paths, encounters, and active invitations are presented separately at [public.portussophia.com](https://public.portussophia.com), allowing the programme and its Founder to remain reciprocal without absorbing one another.

James is selectively available for invited talks, advisory work, consulting, commissioned inquiry, research collaboration, fellowship, publishing, and institutional relationships.
