This is **PortusSophia™**. The most important part of this project is that it was born from another’s belief and faith in me—by which she subscribes **love and acceptance** to her higher-power. The rest is my *best effort to keep it alive*. **PortusSophia™** exists to address a recurring failure in human systems: moving from *what is* toward *what ought to be* without distortion, coercion, or drift. It is not a belief system, and it does not claim truth. It offers **structure, boundaries, and governance primitives** so meaning can remain legible under pressure. **PortusSophia™** is offered in *gratitude, not authority*—so that **dignity, care, and coherence.** - [ORCID.ORG : Connecting Research and Researchers-James RoyDennis](https://orcid.org/0009-0000-6689-8763 "ORCID.ORG : Connect Research and Researchers-James Roy Dennis") [SSRN: Social Science Research Network: Author Page-James Roy Dennis](https://papers.ssrn.com/Sol3/Cf_Dev/AbsByAuth.cfm?per_id=9648792 "SSRN Author Page-James Roy Dennis") I believe we can choose **Love, Faith and Joy — *Here and Now!***
