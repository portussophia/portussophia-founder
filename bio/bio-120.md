James Roy Dennis is an independent researcher, independent scholar, author, and the Founder of PortusSophia, LLC. He develops provenance-centered forms of inquiry for questions that cross disciplinary, professional, and institutional boundaries. His work examines how claims, methods, categories, and evidence move between fields—and how their dependencies, limits, and originating conditions can remain visible.

His applied lineage includes systems, terminology, documentation, healthcare interoperability, engineering, operations, and high-consequence environments. These continuities inform his emphasis on traceable reasoning, bounded authority, and accountable human judgment. He treats AI as an instrument within inquiry rather than an autonomous source of authority.

James’s books, papers, companion inquiries, and public work form the human provenance of PortusSophia, a field architecture for making cross-field encounter governable.
