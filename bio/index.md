James Roy Dennis develops forms of inquiry for questions that exceed the explanatory reach of a single discipline, profession, or institutional frame. His work is concerned with provenance, recursive inquiry, field boundaries, and the conditions under which knowledge can move across contexts without losing its history, limits, or accountability.

He is the Founder of PortusSophia, LLC and the architect of PortusSophia™, a field architecture for making cross-field encounter governable. The programme is separately presented at [public.portussophia.com](https://public.portussophia.com); this site remains the portfolio of the person whose inquiry and professional lineage gave rise to it.

His research posture is independent by design. It brings philosophical inquiry into contact with systems practice, terminology, documentation, healthcare interoperability, engineering, operations, and other high-consequence environments. That applied lineage matters because the central questions are not merely conceptual: they concern how claims, dependencies, translations, and failures become visible in real work.

Across papers, books, companion series, and public writing, James explores the difference between a visible failure and its originating condition. He treats AI as a bounded instrument within accountable human inquiry—not as an autonomous source of authority. The result is a practice that favors traceable reasoning, explicit boundaries, and useful encounters over inflated synthesis.

James is available selectively for invited talks, advisory work, consulting, commissioned inquiry, research collaboration, fellowship, and institutional relationships.
