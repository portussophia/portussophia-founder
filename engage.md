Engagements on this site concern relationships with James and are scoped around a defined question, audience, or institutional need.

## Invited talks

Talks and conversations on provenance, recursive inquiry, cross-field work, AI as a bounded research instrument, and the human origins of PortusSophia.

## Advisory work and consulting

Focused advisory or consulting relationships involving research architecture, terminology, documentation, knowledge boundaries, systems interpretation, or complex cross-domain questions.

## Commissioned inquiry

Bounded research undertaken around a clearly stated paper, problem, domain, or institutional question, with sources, assumptions, and limits made explicit.

## Research collaboration

Collaboration with researchers, authors, practitioners, and institutions where the terms of encounter and the authority of each field can remain visible.

## Fellowship and institutional relationships

Conversations concerning fellowships, residencies, publishing, speaking, and institutional affiliations compatible with an independent research posture.

To begin a James-facing conversation, write to [research@portussophia.com](mailto:research@portussophia.com) or [contact@portussophia.com](mailto:contact@portussophia.com).
