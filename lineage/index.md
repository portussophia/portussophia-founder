  <article class="lineage-card">
    <h2>Technical service and leadership</h2>
    <p>U.S. Army technical service as a Helicopter Missile Systems Electrician (AH-64 Apache), together with NCO leadership and training, developed procedural fidelity, role differentiation, inspection discipline, and responsibility under constraint.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="lineage-card">
    <h2>Human systems and training</h2>
    <p>Organizational leadership, training, conflict handling, and role development made the difference between process compliance and the preservation of people visible. Those capacities later informed differentiated stewardship and conduct-bound criticism.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="lineage-card">
    <h2>Healthcare interfaces</h2>
    <p>Help-desk and clinical-system interface work required following a failure across user, support, application, and operational boundaries. The screen where trouble appeared was rarely the whole path by which a person became stranded.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="lineage-card">
    <h2>Terminology and interoperability</h2>
    <p>Work with LOINC, RELMA, biomedical terminology, and healthcare interoperability strengthened attention to schemas, semantic translation, provenance, repeatability, and the conditions under which meaning survives transfer.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="lineage-card">
    <h2>Enterprise software and stabilization</h2>
    <p>Enterprise software engineering, legacy-system recovery, testing, systems integration, and troubleshooting under incomplete information required recovering implicit knowledge and making complex environments operable for others.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="lineage-card">
    <h2>Documentation and knowledge transfer</h2>
    <p>Technical documentation, setup guidance, training materials, and operational handoffs became a form of interface design: externalizing what a system otherwise required one person to remember.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="lineage-card">
    <h2>Build, release, and reproducibility</h2>
    <p>Automation, professional packaging, repeatable validation, and reproducible environments replaced fragile manual knowledge with inspectable workflows. No unverified counts, timings, or performance figures are asserted here.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="lineage-card">
    <h2>AI-assisted predecessor work</h2>
    <p>Earlier human-directed AI development used modular roles, test-first iteration, reproducible environments, and repeated correction. It is an inspectable technical and methodological predecessor—not, by itself, the later PortusSophia field architecture.</p>
  </article>
