James is not seeking employment with another company. The work already exists. The relevant question is whether a bounded relationship can be formed around a declared inquiry, audience, or institutional need while preserving clear ownership, responsibility, and limits.

## Begin with scope

A useful opening note identifies the question or situation, the people affected, the intended audience, any material deadline, and what kind of relationship is being considered. A first exchange can determine whether the work is appropriately bounded and whether James or PortusSophia is the relevant participant.
