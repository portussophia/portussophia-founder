## Provenance-centered recursive inquiry

James's inquiry mode traces how decisions, distinctions, assumptions, translations, authority, and failures move across interfaces. Each finding becomes a way to expose the next hidden condition. The work may involve thought experiments, gap mapping, requirements and claim provenance, adversarial examination, or reconstruction of a decision path under incomplete information.

The recognizable components—systems thinking, root-cause analysis, interface analysis, exploratory testing, failure tracing, research synthesis—are useful names. The distinctive capacity is their configuration and the recursive movement among them.

## Visible failure is not the originating condition

A conventional engineering question asks, *Where did the system fail?* James more often asks:

> What sequence of decisions, translations, interfaces, and assumptions made this failure possible—and what did the system stop carrying along the way?

Many apparent interface failures are **carry failures**. A value survives while its meaning disappears. One team's “approved” becomes another team's “ready.” A technical state quietly acquires policy standing. An error message reports the final symptom while the stranded person and the originating condition disappear from view.

The inquiry follows the path upstream, then tests how each proposed explanation carries its own burden.

  <h2 id="problem-heading">The problem is yours to declare</h2>
  <p>PortusSophia does not impose a universal problem statement on a person or institution. An empty problem statement was never an omission to be filled by authority; it preserved the place in which the participant names what they believe they are carrying.</p>

## Founder origination and construction

James began with an open field of inquiry. He supplied the originating questions and thought experiments; introduced and corrected distinctions; established Dignity, Respect, and Kindness as the conduct conditions; formulated foundational axioms; mapped recurring gaps; and directed repeated cycles of exploration, correction, integration, and restraint.

He built the inquiry and its architecture. That does not mean he drafted every sentence or that other contributors were incidental. It means the site, purpose, foundational form, research direction, and admission decisions remained Founder-originated and Founder-adjudicated.

## Differentiated stewardship

James designed and operated a stewardship environment in which AI systems held different responsibilities, evaluative perspectives, failure sensitivities, and known drift behaviors. The structure was intended to make disagreement useful rather than to manufacture consensus.

<ol class="process-steps">
  <li><strong>Differentiate roles.</strong> Drafting, criticism, harmonization, standing review, and strategic translation do not carry the same authority.</li>
  <li><strong>Preserve disagreement.</strong> A conflict may disclose an ambiguity, hidden assumption, missing source, or category error.</li>
  <li><strong>Correct drift.</strong> Agent output is revised, redirected, deferred, or rejected when it exceeds the evidence or departs from the declared architecture.</li>
  <li><strong>Keep contribution visible.</strong> AI systems contributed substantively to drafting, research assistance, criticism, comparison, organization, and review.</li>
  <li><strong>Retain human adjudication.</strong> No AI system serves as final arbiter, independent validator, claim owner, or source of authority.</li>
</ol>

Correction is part of the method. So are criticism, uncertainty, and the decision to leave a question unresolved when closure has not been earned.
